About

99 Steps & 7 Falls is a simple 2D Dungeon Crawler where the user can explore and collect items and monsters. In the game, the player will be able to explore through a dungeon entirely made up of text and graphs. They will have to explore through this dungeon with only a limited number of steps, and have to make it to the end and back before they run out of steps. While traveling through the dungeon, the player is allowed to have 4 items on hand. When the player leaves the dungeon, they can keep whatever items they were carrying, and take them into the next run.
